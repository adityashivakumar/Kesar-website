# Content needed from Kesar before launch

Nothing in this build invents client names, certifications, years of
experience, production capacity, MOQ, factory size, employee count,
material purity, delivery timelines, or testimonials. Placeholder copy
says so explicitly where it appears. Before launch, Kesar should supply
and approve:

## Company (About page)
- Official company description, history, founding year, location
- Team information, facility/factory details
- Manufacturing capabilities, certifications (if any)

## Precious metals (Precious Metal page)
- Exact materials, gold/silver specifications, alloys, grades
- Available finishes and case materials
- Customization options

## Manufacturing (Capabilities pages)
- Production capabilities, MOQ, typical timelines
- Quality control process
- Component capabilities in more detail

## Aevum
- Official brand story, product catalogue, watch specifications
- Collection names, photography
- Pricing/enquiry method
- (Full brand site is out of scope for this build — see README)

## Tarini925
- Brand story, jewellery catalogue, photography
- B2B information

## Projects
- Approved client/project names (only ones cleared for public use)
- Images, scope, materials, process, outcomes

## Photography (all pages)
- Product: gold/silver watches, macro shots, cases, dials, movements,
  bracelets, components
- Human: hands, assembly, inspection, design, craftsmanship, finishing
- Manufacturing: equipment, workstations, production floor
- Lifestyle: finished watches, wrist shots

## Analytics & domain
- Confirm production domain (currently set to kesardesigns.com in
  `astro.config.mjs`)
- Analytics provider, if any (PRD §41 lists the recommended event set)
